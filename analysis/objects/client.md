# Client (app instance)

**Purpose:** Record of an installed [Android app](../components/android-app.md), for sync bookkeeping, showing connected phones, and detecting that the owner is away (different time zone).

## Attributes
| Attribute | Type | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `id` | UUID | Generated by the app |
| `user_id` | string | HA user, from the validated HA token |
| `name` | string | e.g. device name |
| `app_version` | string | |
| `time_zone` | string (IANA) | The phone's current time zone, reported on every sync |
| `last_sync_seq` | int | Last `change_seq` the client confirmed |
| `last_seen_at` | datetime UTC | |

## Rules
- The API works without a registered client, but the app always registers and updates its record on every sync (needed for away detection).
- A user only sees their own clients (admins all, read-only). Removing a client does not revoke its login; tokens are revoked in the HA user profile.
- The app keeps a **local copy** of the user's alarms. The server is the source of truth for actions and scheduling; the phone is the source of truth for ringing.
- A phone's time zone is personal data (it reveals travel); it is redacted in logs and debug output.

### Away
- The owner is *away* when the `time_zone` of their most recently seen client differs from the HA time zone.
- While away, the phone rings at its local wall-clock `time`; HA runs **no** actions for that owner and records them as `skipped` ("user away"); the panel shows an away banner; the phone sends no dismiss/snooze reports.
- Normal behaviour resumes as soon as the phone reports the HA time zone again. Known limit: a phone that has not synced since travelling is not detected.

### Sync
- Every change to any alarm (including system changes and deletions) takes the next value of one store-wide `change_seq`. Deletions are kept as tombstones for at least 30 days.
- `GET /sync?since=N` returns alarms with `change_seq > N`, ids deleted since then, the current `change_seq`, and the default snooze. A `since` older than the tombstone window gets a full resync.
- Conflicts: a write carrying an old `revision` gets 409; the app refetches and shows the current version.
- Offline dismiss/snooze reports are queued and sent later (see [OccurrenceEvent](occurrence-event.md) for the late-report rule).
- Background sync runs at most about every 15 minutes, so a change in the panel can take that long to reach the phone. The panel shows each phone's last sync time.

## Decisions
| Decision | Why |
|---|---|
| Sync is **polling** in v1 (`GET /sync`); push later | Simplicity; the contract does not change |
| Sync cursor is the store-wide `change_seq`, not the per-alarm `revision` | Per-alarm counters cannot be compared across alarms; system changes must reach the phone |
| **Away by time zone:** a phone in another time zone rings at local time and HA runs no actions for that owner | A user in another time zone is not at home, so heating etc. is pointless |
